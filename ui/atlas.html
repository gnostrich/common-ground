Candidate census, measured before spending

{{CENSUS}}
chart pairby declarationby depth-1 subtree

Declaration granularity is one declaration seen in two charts — a doc comment and the thing it sits on. Depth-1 subtree lets a document’s position assert its scope, which is far larger and far looser: most of it is prose against prose, where “one directory” can mean a whole repository of markdown. A pair that shows no candidates at all is a real absence, not a gap in the measurement.
